In addition to sticks, twigs and stones, you may also encounter small pieces of ores scattered around on the ground. These pieces are important, as they are your only source of ore before obtaining a pickaxe.

These small ore pieces serve two purposes: being sources of metal, and indicating the presence of a larger vein nearby. Be sure to note down small ores, as the location of ore veins will be useful later during prospecting.

There are 12 types of small ores you can find:
- Native Copper (Copper)
- Native Gold (Gold)
- Hematite (Cast Iron)
- Native Silver (Silver)
- Cassiterite (Tin)
- Bismuthinite (Bismuth)
- Garnierite (Nickel)
- Malachite (Copper)
- Limonite (Cast Iron)
- Sphalerite (Zinc)
- Tetrahedrite (Copper)


# Casting
In TFC, every ore contains a certain amount of units, or **mB (millibuckets)** of actual metal which can be extracted. Small ores found on the surface are the lowest quality, only providing **16 mB** of metal. 

In order to extract this metal, it needs to be **melted**, and made into tools using a process called **casting**.

You will need a [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/The world/Resources/Clay#Knapping clay|small vessel]], [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/The world/Surviving/Heat and fire#Pit kilns|pit kiln]], one or more [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/The world/Resources/Clay#Knapping clay|mold(s)]], and at least 144 mB of a suitable metal for casting: copper.

There are four types of copper (alloys) you can use:
- Copper
- Bronze (20-30% tin, 70-80% copper)
- Bismuth bronze (20-30% zinc, 10-20% bismuth, 50-65% copper)
- Black bronze (10-25% silver, 10-25% gold, 50-70% copper)

Bismuth bronze is the easiest bronze to make, but its also the weakest. Black bronze is the best early game, but also the most expensive. Copper and bronze are both average.

## The casting process
Firstly, collect the ores you want to melt down. Put them in the **small vessel** and make sure you put in the right amount in case you want to alloy. Be also sure to not overfull it, as it can only hold up to 3024 mB of liquid, excess will be voided!

Next, place the vessel in the [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/The world/Surviving/Heat and fire#Pit kilns|pit kiln]] and heat it up. The vessel will heat up and eventually melt down.

The next steps will need a bit of a hurry, as the metal is cooling down and will solidify eventually. If this happens, you need to re-heat it again.

Be careful when you pick up the vessel as its really hot! Craft **wooden tongs** using two sticks/twigs and a knife. Putting this in your offhand will prevent you from burning when you put up the vessel.<br>![[Vault-data/Attachments/Ores, metal and casting wooden tongs.png]]

Hold the vessel in your hand and right click to open up the **casting interface**. Inside this interface, drag your mold of choice into the center slot and wait until it has filled with liquid metal.

Once the mold it full, it can be left to cool. The contents can be extracted by putting it into a crafting grid as soon as it has cooled down.

Congrats! you just created your first metal tool head!
