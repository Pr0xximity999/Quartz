Clay is an extremely useful item throughout this entire pack. Early on it is used for pottery (molds, pots, jugs…) 

# Locating and collecting clay
Locating it can be tricky at first, since it is usually hidden by grass. Clay is often found in two locations:
- Areas with at least 175 mm annual rainfall
- Near riverbeds, lakes or ponds in smaller deposits

In areas with at least 175 mm of annual rainfall (as seen in the temperature section in your inventory), clay can be found in patches all over the place, usually marked by certain plants, known as *clay indicators*. Finding any of these plants means there is clay nearby.

These clay indicators are:
- Athyrium fern
- Canna (not shown)
- Goldenrod
- Pampas grass
- Perovskia
- Water canna

# Knapping clay
Just like [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/The world/Resources/Sticks 'n stones#Knapping|rocks]], clay can be knapped to form new items, which costs 5 clay. Though unlike stone, messing up a clay recipe doesn’t make you lose a piece of clay.

After knapping a clay recipe, it needs to be fired before it can be used. Firing is the process of heating the item up to a point where the clay will turn into a hard **ceramic** material. This process requires heating to 1400 degrees Celsius, or [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/Surviving/Heat and fire#Temperature values|Yellow White]]. Firing is usually done in the [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/Surviving/Heat and fire#Pit kiln|pit kiln]].
