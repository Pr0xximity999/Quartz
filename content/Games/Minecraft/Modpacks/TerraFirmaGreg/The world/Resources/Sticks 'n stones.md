# Gathering sticks and stones
Starting off with TerraFirmaCraft (TFC) is very different from vanilla minecraft. You cannot mine trees with your bare hands.

Around you on the ground lay sticks and stones. Break or press right click to pick them up. You can also gather sticks by breaking leaves.

# Knapping
To create an axe, you will need to gather at least 1 stick and 2 rocks. Hold at least two rocks in your hand and press right click to enter the **knapping** interface. Knapping is the act of smashing rocks together to form them into crude shapes.

This interface will return a number of times with a handful of materials like clay, leather or straw.

![[Vault-data/Attachments/Sticks 'n stones knapping interaface.png]]<br>The interface will look like this.

Use the JEI terminal to look up a stone axe head recipe. Keep in mind that messing up the recipe will cost you one stone.

![[Vault-data/Attachments/Sticks 'n stones stone axe head.png]]<br>The axe head shape, as per the handbook. Combine it with a string to get an axe to start cutting down trees.

>[!warning] Trees hurt
>In this pack, trees fall down when you break them. Make sure to not stand underneath one or you will get seriously hurt.
