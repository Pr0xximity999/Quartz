Fire is a very important technological advancement. You won’t get far in life without it.

# Heating
Heating items is the act of converting one item to another, or a fluid. Items can be heated in many ways, as specified in this article. A [[#Firepits|firepit]] or[[#Pit kilns|pit kiln]] to name a few.

However, all these methods function in the same way. You place some flammable items together and it will slowly start to heat up, as shown in the items’s tooltip.

## Temperature values
The temperature of an item is represented by a color, which will changes throughout the following values:
- **Warming**: 1 - 80 °C
- **Hot**: 80 - 210 °C
- **Very Hot**: 210 - 480 °C
- **Faint Red**: 480 - 580 °C
- **Dark Red**: 580 - 730 °C
- **Bright Red**: 730 - 930 °C
- **Orange**: 930 - 1100 °C
- **Yellow**: 1100 - 1300 °C
- **Yellow White**: 1300 - 1400 °C
- **White**: 1400 - 1500 °C
- **Brilliant White**: >1500 °C

Picking up an item with any value above **Hot** will result in you dropping the item and being burnt a little, unless you hold a pair of tongs in your offhand (like wooden tongs).

# Creating fire
Creating a fire will be done using a **firestarter**. In order to use a firestarter, hold down right click on the ground. After a few moments smoke and then fire will appear. It might take a few tries before the fire will light successfully.

![[Vault-data/Attachments/Heat firestarter recipe.png]]<br>A firestarter can be created using any two sticks or twigs.

# Firepits
Now that you have a firestarter, it is possible to create a **firepit**. In order to create a firepit, you will need one **log**, three **sticks**, and optionally op to five pieces of **kindling**. Kindling can be items suck as paper products, straw or pinecones, and will increase the chance of successfully creating a firepit by 10 percent per item.

Throw all the items on the ground on the same block and use the firestarter on the same block. If all went well, you should have created a firepit.

![[Vault-data/Attachments/Heat firepit.png]]

Right clicking the firepit will open the firepit menu. On the left are four **fuel** slots, which can be filled with logs, peat and stick bundles (don’t google that name). Either shift-clicking or placing any fuel item in the top left slot will insert it into the fuel slot.

Fuel will be consumed from the bottommost slot. There is a gauge which displays the current **[[#Temperature values|Temperature]]** of the firepit and on the right there is a slot to enter items to be heated in.

![[Vault-data/Attachments/Heat firepit menu.png]]
## Crafting items
Many items can be made in a firepit by heating them(at warming [[#Temperature values|temperatures]]). Sticks can be heated to turn them into two **torches**. Note that torches will eventually burn out and need to be re-lit by using a firestarter or another torch on them.

The firepit can also be used to cook food (at hot [[#Temperature values|temperatures]]). All raw meats and doughs can be cooked in a firepit, which will also lengthen their [[|shelf life]] (spoiling duration).

Additionally, an **iron grill** or **ceramic pot** can be used on the firepit to transform it into a **Grill** or **Pot** respectively.  
### Grill
A grill can be used to cook foods more efficiently, being able to hold up to five items at once and also giving food the **Wood Grilled** trait after being cooked (which will give a minor buff to the item’s [[|expiration date]]). A grill is created by working a **Wrought Iron Double Sheet** on an [[|anvil]].

<br>![[Vault-data/Attachments/Heat and fire grill interface.png]]

### Pot
A pot is an item that can be used to cook new types of food and also produce some other useful items. A ceramic pot must be [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/The world/Resources/Clay#Knapping clay|knapped]] out of clay first, and then [[#Pit kilns|fired]].

A pot has five slots for crafting and up to 1000 mB for fluids. In order to cook something, the pot must be filled with a fluid using any type of fluid container like a jug or bucket. Use the fluid container on the firepit to add it. Afterwards, add items and light the pot. It will begin boiling for a while until the recipe is completed.

![[Vault-data/Attachments/Heat and fire pot interface.png]]

Soup recipes are made from 3-5 fruits, vegetables or meats with water. When the recipe is done, right click the pot with a bowl to retrieve it.

Other pot recipes transform the items and fluid in the pot into something else. For example: boiling five ash in water makes **lye**.
## Breaking a firepit
To remove either device, shift right click on the firepit, but be careful to not remove it while they’re still hot!.

## Fuel purity and smoke
Firepit fuels have different levels of purity. Higher purity will make hotter fires and less smoke, while impure items will make the fire more smokey. Logs are the purest fuel, with pine being the least pure. Things like leaves and pinecones are very impure and are not hot enough for cooking, but create a lot of smoke.

Firepits can be extinguished using a shovel (break it), which will drop some ash and any placed devices.
# Pit kilns
A pit kiln is an early game method of [[#Heating|heating]] items up. For example, it can be used to **fire** [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/The world/Resources/Clay|clay]] into ceramic. The pit kiln will heat its contents up to 1400 °C, or **Yellow White**, over the course of eight hours (8 in game minutes).

To build a pit kiln you’ll need:
- up to 4 items to be fired
- 8 pieces of straw
- 8 logs
- An item capable of lighting fires, like a [[#Creating fire|firestarter]] or a torch

>[!note]
>Torches can start fires simply by tossing the torch on a pit kiln and waiting a few seconds.

# Charcoal forges
Out of al the methods listed here, the charcoal forge is the best way to heat up stuff. They’re usually used for smithing purposes with an anvil.

## Building the forge
To create a charcoal forge, you can place down 5 stone blocks of any type filled with [[Games/Minecraft/Modpacks/TerraFirmaGreg/The world/Resources/Charcoal|charcoal]] in the middle (consisting of 7 or 8 layers), which is then lit. 

Bellows can be placed adjacent and one block up to the forge in order to raise the maximum temperature.

![[Vault-data/Attachments/Heat and fire charcoal forge.png]]

But that’s not all, the forge needs a chimney in order to work properly.

The block above the forge middle needs to be either air or a crucible. Next to that, either the block directly above the air block, or any of 8 nearby blocks (horizontally) need to see the sky.

Here’s a visualization which blocks i mean by that:<br>![[Vault-data/Attachments/Heat and fire forge chimney.png]]

Any of the blue stained glass blocks need to see the sky.

## Using the forge
Clicking the forge opens up an inventory interface that looks something like this:<br>![[Vault-data/Attachments/Heat and fire forge interface.png]]

The bottom 5 slots in the v pattern are used for fuel(which can be either coal or charcoal), items that are to be molten can be placed in the upper 5 slots of the v pattern, and finally, any item placed in the right column will be filled up when stuff gets molten (like vessels and molds).
