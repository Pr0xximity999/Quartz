# Rock layers
The world is divided up into different types of rock. These regions can be multiple kilometers across, and will usually consist of two or three layers. Different ores can be found in different rock types, so it’s important that you’re able to locate specific rock types.

The top layer of rock on a continent will either be [[#igneous extrusive]], or [[#Sedimentary rock|sedimentary]]. Underneath sedimentary rocks will likely be [[#Metamorphic rock|metamorphosed]] forms of the rock above.

For example: marble (a metamorphic rock) will likely be found under limestone or chalk. High grade metamorphic rocks are found deep under other metamorphic or igneous rocks.

## Ocean floors
Ocean floors are composed of [[#Igneous extrusive]] rock: rocks that are formed by magma quickly cooling. Underneath there will likely be a [[#Igneous intrusive]] rock of the same [[#Rock grades|grade]]. 

For example: under basalt (a mafic, igneous extrusive rock) will likely be gabbro (a mafic igneous intrusive rock).

## Uplift regions
In mountainous areas regions you might also see uplift, where a [[#Metamorphic rock|metamorphic]] or [[#Igneous rock|igneous]] rock is found on the surface.

Uplift rocks can be found above other continental [[#Sedimentary rock|sedimentary]] or higher-grade metamorphic rocks.

In addition, [dikes (geology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dike_%28geology%29): small vertical slices of igneous intrusive rock, may appear around the world, protruding trough the upper layers of rocks. They cut across older rock beds and are filled when magma fills a fracture in older beds (magmatic dikes specifically). 

With all that in mind, there are four types of rocks (already mentioned throughout the previous chapters): sedimentary, metamorphic, igneous extrusive, and igneous intrusive. These categories will determine where what rock and what ore will spawn.
## Rock types

### Sedimentary rock
[Sedimentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentary_rock) rocks are formed by the accumulation or deposition(cementation) of mineral or organic particles (sediments, geological and biological detrius) . Sedimentation is any process that causes these particles to settle. Geological detrius originates from weathering and erosion of existing rocks (carried by water), or from the solidification of molten lava blobs erupted by volcanoes.

They are typically found on the top of layers of rock in continental areas. 

Some sedimentary rocks are:
- Shale
- Claystone
- Limestone
- Conglomerate
- Dolomite
- Chert
- Chalk

### Metamorphic rock
[Metamorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metamorphic_rock) rocks are created by a process called **metamorphism**. They can be found underneath their corresponding [[#Sedimentary rock|sedimentary]] or [[#Igneous rock|igneous]] rock, or in [[#Uplift regions|uplift areas]].

Some metamorphic rocks are:
- Slate → under shale, claystone and conglomerate
- Marble → under limestone, dolomite and chalk
- Quartzite → under chert
- Phyllite → under slate
- Schist, gneiss → phyllite or other [[#igneous intrusive]] rocks

### Igneous rock
Igneous rocks are formed from magma cooling on or in the earths surface. There are two types of Igneous rock: intrusive and extrusive.
#### Igneous rock grades
igneous rocks can have cerrtain grades, or modifiers that can change some properties about them.
##### Felsic rock
Felsic is a rock modifier describing igneous rock that are relatively rich in elements that form feldspar(rock ground consisting of minerals like aluminium, sodium, calcium, potassium or barium) and quartz. Felsic is a mix of the words **fel**dspar and **sil**i**c**a. It’s opposite version (or contrast) is [[#Mafic rock|mafic rock]].

Felsic rocks are usually light in color. The most common felsic rock is granite.

Felsic minerals are:
- Quartz
- Sodium
- Aluminium
- Potassium

Felsic rocks are (in game):
- Granite
- Rhyolite

##### Mafic rock
Mafic is a rock modifier describing igneous rock made from a silicate mineral rich in magnesium and iron. Mafic is a mix of the words **ma**gnesium and **fe**rr**ic**.

Mafic rocks are usually dark in color. Mafic minerals can also be described as **ferromagnesian**

Mafic minerals are:
- Olivine
- Pyroxene
- Amphibole
- Biotite

Mafic rocks are:
- Basalt
- Diabase (not in game)
- Gabbro

##### Intermediate rock
Intermediate rock a mix of [[#Mafic rock|mafic]] and [[#Felsic rock|felsic]] rock.

Intermediate rocks are:
- Andesite
- Dacite
- Diorite
- Trachyandesite (not in game)

#### Igneous intrusive
[Igneous intrusive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igneous_rock#Intrusive) rocks are formed from magma which cooled and solidifies *within* the earth’s crust. 

Bodies of intrusive rock are known as **intrusions** and are surrounded by pre-existing rock called [country rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_rock_(geology)), which is the rock native to wherever the intrusions…intrude. The magma cools slowly, due to the country rock being a great thermal insulator. This causes the rock to grow mineral grains (phanteric) which are visible to the naked eye.

Igneous intrusive rock can be found in:
- Deep underground
- Dikes (rarely)
- [[#Uplift regions|Uplift areas]] (rarely)

Common igneous intrusive roc are:
- Granite
- Diorite
- Gabbro
#### Igneous extrusive
[Igneous intrusive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igneous_rock#Extrusive) rock (also known as volcanic rock) is formed from magma cooling on the earth’s surface. 

The magma, which is brought to the surface by volcanic eruptions, rapidly solidifies. This causes the rock to grow fine mineral grains (aphantic) or even become glassy. 

Basalt is the most common extrusive igneous rock and forms lava- flows, sheets and plateaus. Basalt sometimes solidifies in long polygonal columns (as seen in minecraft).

Igneous extrusive rock can be found in:
- The top layer of rock in continental areas
- The ocean floor

Common igneous extrusive rocks are:
- Basalt
- Rhyolite
- Andesite
- Dacite
