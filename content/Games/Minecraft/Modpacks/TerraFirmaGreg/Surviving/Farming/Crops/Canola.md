# General info
Canola is a two block tall crop that can be planted on farmland.

Canola crops will produce Canola Ovules as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: -5 - 25°C
> **Hydration**: 10 - 60%
> **Nutrient**: Phosphorus
