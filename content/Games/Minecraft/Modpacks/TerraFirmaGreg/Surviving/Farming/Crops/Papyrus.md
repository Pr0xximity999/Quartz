# General info
Papyrus is a **two** block tall crop that can be planted on **farmland**.

Papyrus can be used to create paper

Papyrus crops will produce Papyrus (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: 19 - 37°C
> **Hydration**: 70 - 100%
> **Nutrient**: Potassium
