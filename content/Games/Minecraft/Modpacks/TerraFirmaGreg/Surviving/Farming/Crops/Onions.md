# General info
 Onions are a **single** block crop a that can be planted on **farmland**.

 Onion crops will produce Onion(seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: 0 - 30°C
> **Hydration**: 25 - 90%
> **Nutrient**: Nitrogen
