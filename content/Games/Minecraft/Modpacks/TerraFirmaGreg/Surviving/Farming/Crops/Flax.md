# General info
 Flax is a two block tall crop that can be planted on farmland.

Wild flax grows between -5 and 27 C and 175 to 475 mm of rainfall.

 Flax crops will product flax(seeds) as a product, which can be used as fibers.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: -8 - 25  °C
> **Hydration**: 15 - 65%
> **Nutrient**: Nitrogen
