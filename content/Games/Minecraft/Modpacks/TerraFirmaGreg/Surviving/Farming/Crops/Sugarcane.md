# General info
Sugarcane is a **two block** tall crop that can be planted on **farmland**.

Sugarcane can be used to craft sugar.

Sugarcane crops will produce sugarcane (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: 12 - 38°C
> **Hydration**: 40 - 100%
> **Nutrient**:  Potassium
