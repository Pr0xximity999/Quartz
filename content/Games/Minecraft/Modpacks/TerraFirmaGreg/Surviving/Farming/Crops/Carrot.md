# General info
Carrot is a **single** block crop that can be planted on **farmland**.

Carrot crops will produce Carrot (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: 3 - 30°C
> **Hydration**: 25 - 100%
> **Nutrient**: Potassium
