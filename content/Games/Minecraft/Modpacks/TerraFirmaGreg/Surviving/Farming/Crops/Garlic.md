# General info
 Garlic is a **single** block crop that can be planted on **farmland**.

 Garlic crops will produce garlic (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: -20 - 18°C
> **Hydration**: 15 - 75%
> **Nutrient**: Nitrogen
