# General info
Wheat is a **single** block crop that can be planted on **farmland**.

Wheat crops will produce wheat (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: -4 - 35°C
> **Hydration**: 25 - 100%
> **Nutrient**: Phosphorus
