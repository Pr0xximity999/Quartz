# General info
Maize is a **two** block tall crop that can be planted on farmland.

Maize crops will produce Maize (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: 13 - 40°C
> **Hydration**: 75 - 100%
> **Nutrient**: Phosphorus
