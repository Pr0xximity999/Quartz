# General info
Tomatoes are a **climbing two** block tall crop that can be planted on farmland.

Sticks are required for tomatoes to grow fully.

Tomato crops will produce tomato (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: 0 - 36°C
> **Hydration**: 30 - 95%
> **Nutrient**: Potassium
