# General info
Rye is a **single** block that can be planted on **farmland**.

Rye crops will produce rye (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: -11 - 30°C
> **Hydration**:  25 - 85%
> **Nutrient**: Phosphorus
