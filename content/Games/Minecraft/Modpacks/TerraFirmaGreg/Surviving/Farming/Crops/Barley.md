# General info
Barley is a single block crop that can be planted on farmland.

Barley crops will produce barley(seeds) as a product, which will give grains when eaten.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: -8 - 26 °C
> **Hydration**: 18 - 75%
> **Nutrient**: Nitrogen
