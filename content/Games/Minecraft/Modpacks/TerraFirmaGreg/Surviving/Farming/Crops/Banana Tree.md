# General info
Bananas are a special kind of fruit tree. They grown only vertically, lack leaves, and only fruit at the topmost block.

Saplings are dropped from the flowering part of the plant.

Once it has been harvested, it dies and will not produce any more fruit. It must be replanted in the spring.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: 17 - 35°C
> **Rainfall**: 280 - 500mm
