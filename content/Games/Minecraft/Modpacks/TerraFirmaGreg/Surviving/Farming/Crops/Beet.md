# General info
Beets are a single block crop that can be planted on.farmland

Beet crops will produce beet (seeds) as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: -5 - 20°C
> **Hydration**: 18 - 85%
> **Nutrient**: Potassium
