# General info
Pumpkins are a **spreading** crop that can be planted on **farmland** and will place up to **two** pumpkin blocks on the ground next to it while it is mature.

Pumpkins can be turned into Jack ‘o Lanterns

Pumpkin crops will produce Pumpkin seeds as a product.

>[!success] Requirements
> **Temperature**: 0 - 30°C
> **Hydration**: 30 - 80%
> **Nutrient**: Phosphorus
