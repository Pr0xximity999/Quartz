>**DEI**: Diversity, Equity, Inclusion
>**CSR**: Company Social Responsibility

DEI means that there is no discrimination between people and that everyone has equal opportunities unregardless of how they are 
# Biggest benefits of DEI
DEI encourages more innovation, creativity and problem solving (See Lewis Model of Communication differences below). It creates a sense of ownership and a stronger corporate culture when people work for the same goal and provides greater opportunities for everyone involved.
