# What is a system?
Systems are two or more parts that form a whole. Everything around you is a system. 
A system is something interconnected which makes the whole better than the sum of its parts. When a part of a system breaks or gets re-arranged, it might influence the system.
A group of things that do not interconnect or interact is called a heap/collection.
