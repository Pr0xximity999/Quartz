# Hard skills
> **Hard skills**: Employee’s ability to do a specific task
- Specialized knowledge
- Technical abilities
	
Hard skills which are in demand of the work life
- Data analytics
- Research skills
- Technological & Digital skills
- Project Management
- industry specific technical expertise
- Remote works & Digital Collaboration tools
- Green skills & Sustainability knowledge

# Soft skills
> **Soft skills**: way to do a certain task
- Behavior and thinking, personal traits, cognitive skills
- Difficult to measure
- provide a possibility to thrive in different roles and industries

Soft skills which are in demand of the work life 
> Global study conducted on LinkedIn in 2024 with 1 billion responses
- Communication
- Collaboration & Teamwork
- Critical thinking & Analytical thinking → core skill to problem solving and innovations
- Emotional intelligence including:
	- Self-awareness
	- Self-regulation
	- social skills
	- listening skills
	- empathy
	- motivation
	- recognition of emotions
	- etc…
- Adaptability, Agility & Life-long learning
- Leadership & People management
- Customer service orientation

Soft skills are becoming the main deciding factor whether you will be hired or not and advancing in your career. Soft skills develop gradually, you won’t improve them over night.

# A good story
- A clear core (what is it about? summarized in one sentence)
- Compelling characters (want something, have flaws, make choices, change)
- Conflict and stakes (External and internal)
	- What happens if the character fails
	- External = society, nature, enemies
	- Internal = fear, guilt, desire
- Cause and effect (not just events)
	- Each event causes the next one
- Change or revelation (in the end something must be different or otherwise the story is pointless)
- Emotional truth
	- Emotions must be true even if details weren’t
- structure
	- The purpose of guiding attention and getting delivering payoff
- voice and specificity
