# The problem
Ok, so. Cybersecurity. Preferably offensive.

Avans does teach it, but only blue-teaming; how to make cybersafe applications and how to program safe, basically. No offensive cybersecurity.

It can be handy to gain insight, but it ultimately does not teach the right skills and toolset.

# The solution
One way to fix this would be to learn cybersec at the side while doing the education. This would work but is still sub-optimal as i have to allocate most of my attention towards school.

The **optimal** route would be to look for an organisation or business that would hire me to train me on offensive cybersecurity with a part time education…and drop out of Avans after *finishing this year*.

# The challenge

## Looking for a place to work and study
First things first: **Looking for a work/learning place**. Positions that want ethical hackers, penetration testers, or just red-teamers have a lot of different requirements. Additionally, companies don’t always have the opportunity to give you part time education, but maybe only a (online) course or something. Having some form of valid schooling is important for me as i need to **finish a course to not have to repay my student debt**.

## Skill issue
Secondly, showing **you’re competent**.
Some want to see an **HBO certificate**. gotta get that check-mark, i suppose. But not all. Some positions just want the “*HBO denkwijze*”, which just means you need to have a certain level of thinking.

Next, a lot want to see **cybersecurity certificates** (OSCP, GPEN, OSCE, etc..). *Having at least one* of these is recommended, but getting multiple shows the desire to grow.

Personally, im not that good at cybersecurity. Ive only completed 3 easy HTB boxes: [[Personal-projects/Hack The Box/Boxes/1 Facts - Easy box|Facts]], [[Personal-projects/Hack The Box/Boxes/2 Conversor - Easy box|Conversor]], and [[Personal-projects/Hack The Box/Boxes/3 WingData - Easy box|Wingdata]]. So yeah, gotta lock in. 
Next to that, its highly adviced to learn to **document**. Make **write-ups** about the boxes, write **blogs** and generally try to understand the vulnerabilities and just **expand your knowledge** on exploitation, enumeration and documentation.

Currently, getting a certificate is not really possible due to my skill level being too low, so firstly ill just focus on Hack The Box, and maybe doing bug bounties.
