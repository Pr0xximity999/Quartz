# The sum of two numbers
Adding two numbers together is called the **sum**.
The sum is usually mathematically denoted as a plus symbol: $+$
The two numbers used in the sum are called the **terms**.

For example: 
- The sum of 2 and 6 is: $2 + 6$
	- 2 and 6 are the terms
- The sum of 1 and 3 is: $1 + 3$
	- 1 and 3 are the terms
- The sum of 3 and 4 is: $3 + 4$
	- 3 and 4 are the terms
# The difference of two numbers
Subtracting two numbers from each other is called the **difference**.
The difference is usually mathematically denoted as a minus symbol: $-$
The two numbers used in the sum are called the **terms**.

For example: 
- The difference between 6 and 2 is: $6 - 2$
    - 6 and 2 are the terms
- The difference between 5 and 3 is: $5 - 3$
    - 5 and 3 are the terms
- The difference between 7 and 4 is: $7 - 4$
    - 7 and 4 are the terms
# The product of two numbers
Multiplying two numbers together is called the **product**.
The product is usually mathematically denoted as an x symbol: $\times$
The two numbers used in a product are called the factors.
The left number of the product will be added together the amount of times set by the right number (or the left number *times* the right number).

For example:
- The product of 2 and 6 is: $2 \times 6$ or $2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2$
	- 2 and 6 are the factors
- The product of 1 and 3 is: $1 \times 3$ or $1 + 1 + 1$
	- 1 and 3 are the factors
- The product of 3 and 4 is: $2 \times 6$ or $3 + 3 + 3 + 3$
	- 3 and 4 are the factors
# The quotient of two numbers
Dividing two numbers by one another is called the **quotient**.
The quotient is usually mathematically denoted as an obelus: $\div$
You can also write divisions as fractions, where the left number goes on the top, and the right number on the bottom

The left number is called the **dividend** (the number being divided)
- the dividend is called the numerator (top) in a fraction
The right number is called the **divisor** (the number you are dividing by)
- The divisor is called the denominator (bottom) in a fraction
Dividing means you will calculate how many times *the divisor(right) fits in the dividend(left)*.

A number is only a divisor of the dividend, if the result is an integer (whole number). This doesn't mean that you can divide the two, it just means that the solution isn't an integer.

For example:
- The quotient of 6 and 2 is: $6 \div 2$
    - 6 is the dividend, and 2 is the divisor
    - Their fraction is: $\frac{6}{2}$
    - 2 is a divisor of 6 because $6 \div 2 = 3$
- The quotient of 12 and 3 is: $12 \div 3$
    - 12 is the dividend, and 3 is the divisor
    - Their fraction is: $\frac{12}{3}$
    - 3 is a divisor of 12 because $12 \div 3 = 4$
- The quotient of 15 and 4 is: $15 \div 4$
    - 15 is the dividend, and 4 is the divisor
    - Their fraction is: $\frac{15}{4}$
    - 4 is *not* a divisor of 15 because $5 \div 4 = 3.75$ (not an whole number)
